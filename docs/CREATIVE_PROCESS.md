# Creative Process — stable purpose, evolving expression

## Three levels of commitment
- Confirmed direction: explicit owner decisions in PRODUCT_VISION and release requirements in LAUNCH_CRITERIA.
- Working hypothesis: a reversible design choice being tested. State the reason and uncertainty.
- Idea bank: possibilities, names, and analogies. Mentioning an idea does not approve implementation or make it a launch gate.

The latest explicit owner instruction supersedes an earlier brief. Historical conversations and old design files are reference material, not automatic requirements. Record conflicts rather than silently blending incompatible concepts.

## Iteration
Choose one user outcome; make a coherent playable slice; collect actual interaction evidence; review; obtain a reaction when available; refine. Do not require the owner to specify screens, exact copy, or an exercise protocol before useful design work can happen.

Agents may choose reversible layout, metaphor, typography, navigation, animation, and internal architecture within the task. Explain the few consequential choices. Avoid asking permission for routine decisions or generating dozens of variants instead of making one considered proposal.

## Owner feedback during work
Treat feedback as steering, not an interruption to resist. Capture new ideas separately from current acceptance criteria. Finish the current bounded cycle against its recorded brief unless the new direction makes that work obsolete or the owner asks to stop; in that case stop and preserve the work. Do not secretly change review criteria halfway through a cycle.

A completed prototype is a candidate, not an approved brand or finished product. When awaiting taste feedback, proceed only on already-authorized independent work; silence is not approval for paid services, publication, or material product changes.

## Review without creative rigidity
Separate blocking defects from optional design suggestions. Reject interactions that undermine confirmed direction, exclude users, make misleading claims, or fail their task. Do not reject a coherent design merely because another visual style is possible. Conversely, a technically functioning screen that teaches nothing through interaction does not satisfy this concept.

A task PASS means its acceptance criteria passed. It does not establish emotional benefit, owner endorsement, clinical effectiveness, or launch readiness.

## Durable records
Maintain a brief decisions log: date, decision, status, reason, and source of owner approval where applicable. Keep sensitive autobiographical details out of app code and public artifacts; use general principles. The orchestrator or supervising agent owns brief updates and evidence records. Implementation agents report proposed changes without editing their own review rules.

## Full-build authorization
The owner now requests the complete app. docs/BUILD_PLAN.md defines the implementation sequence. Continue across milestones; the former single-slice-only stop is superseded. Tests, permission boundaries, and release-only approvals remain in force.
