# Guided Experience Catalog

Brainstorm record, 2026-10-02. These are product concepts, not clinically validated exercise scripts. Titles and detailed flows remain drafts unless explicitly approved.

## Requested by the owner

### Body awareness, wants, and needs — title open
Experience what your body feels to get in touch with your wants and needs. This is ONE guided experience, not the whole app's purpose. Tone should be compelling and inviting. “Feel what you want” was proposed but not approved as the app positioning. Treat bodily sensations as clues to explore alongside context, not infallible instructions.

### Mental overwhelm and rising panic — working title: Find your steady
Owner requested a professionally designed experience for mental chaos, feeling scattered, and calming a panic state. Draft positioning: “Grounding for racing thoughts and rising panic.”

Draft interaction: minimal decisions, one short instruction per screen; orient to surroundings, offer a neutral external anchor or comfortable physical contact, stay with one manageable sensory prompt, then choose to continue, try another approach, pause, or contact a trusted person. Never require inward body focus or guarantee relief. Include an appropriate support route; do not assume unfamiliar physical symptoms are necessarily panic.

Source informing the concept: NHS inform, Grounding exercises — https://www.nhsinform.scot/healthy-living/mental-wellbeing/breathing-and-relaxation-exercises/grounding-exercises/
The specific app sequence remains a design draft requiring content review.

## Shared entry principle
Guide users toward a suitable experience from their present situation; do not require familiarity with techniques. Explain why a suggestion fits, what happens, and realistic expectations. Offer alternatives. “I’m not sure” and “that didn’t help” must have useful, respectful paths.
